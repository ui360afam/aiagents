The Starting Point of Agents of Shield in the Marvel Timeline

Agents of Shield first aired in 2013, set immediately after the events of the film The Avengers, which was released in 2012. The show begins shortly after the Chitauri invasion of New York City. This places the series within the early post-Avengers era of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

The timeline is primarily based on references in the show to events from Marvel movies and continuity. The initial episodes follow Agent Coulson and his team as they navigate the aftermath of the Avengers' battle and the emerging threats to global security.

  • Series premiere: 2013, set post-2012 Avengers
  • Main conflict involves Hydra and other threats
  • References to major Marvel events like Captain America: The Winter Soldier

Key Events Influencing the Timeline

The show references the fall of S.H.I.E.L.D. in Captain America: The Winter Soldier, which occurred in 2014. This event is critical as it causes a major shift in the series' narrative and timeline, leading to the dissolution of S.H.I.E.L.D.

Post-2014, the series explores the rebuilding of S.H.I.E.L.D. and new threats, aligning with the broader MCU developments.

How To Determine the Exact Timing of Agents of Shield

Pinpointing the exact time frame requires examining references in the show, cross-referencing with Marvel films, and noting the progression of story arcs. The series mostly aligns with the timeline between the second and third phases of the MCU.

Season 1 begins shortly after The Avengers, while Season 2 explicitly references the events of Captain America: The Winter Soldier. Seasons 4 and 5 take place around the time of Avengers Infinity War and Endgame, but with some flexibility and narrative shifts.

  • Season 1: 2012-2013, immediately after The Avengers
  • Season 2: 2014, post-Winter Soldier
  • Seasons 4-5: around 2018-2020, during Infinity War and Endgame
  • Final Season: 2020, after Endgame

Exceptions and Edge Cases

The series occasionally introduces time jumps or references to Marvel films that span years, creating some ambiguity. For example, the final season's events are loosely connected to the MCU's Phase Three, but with some narrative flexibility.

Certain episodes feature time travel or alternate realities, which can distort linear chronology. These are typically marked as such and are considered exceptions rather than the rule.
