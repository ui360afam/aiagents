The Evolution of AI Agents

AI agents began as simple rule-based systems in the early 2010s. They evolved into more complex, learning systems around 2015. The rise of cloud computing and big data accelerated their development, making AI agents smarter and more autonomous.

Today, AI agents perform diverse tasks, from customer service bots to virtual assistants. The shift towards agentic AI, systems capable of independent decision-making, marks a significant milestone.

  • Initial AI agents: rule-based, limited scope
  • 2015: Rise of machine learning-powered agents
  • 2018: Introduction of autonomous, agentic AI systems
