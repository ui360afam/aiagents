What Kind of AI Agents Can I Build?

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 1 min read

Many developers and businesses ask, what kind of AI agents can I build? Since AI agents are becoming increasingly capable and popular, understanding the types you can create is essential. AI agents are software systems that perform tasks autonomously, making decisions based on data and algorithms. They are seen as the future because they can automate complex processes, improve efficiency, and adapt to new information. When did AI agents come out? The concept dates back to early AI research in the 1950s, but modern AI agents gained popularity after the rise of machine learning in the 2010s. Now, they are used in customer service, robotics, finance, and more.

Types of AI Agents You Can Build

AI agents vary widely in complexity and purpose. They range from simple rule-based systems to advanced autonomous entities capable of learning and decision-making. Your choice depends on the task, available data, and desired autonomy.

  • Rule-based agents for basic automation
  • Reactive agents that respond to environment stimuli
  • Deliberative agents that plan and reason
  • Learning agents that adapt over time
  • Hybrid agents combining multiple approaches
Type of AgentDescriptionExample
Rule-BasedFollow predefined rules for specific tasksCustomer support chatbots
ReactiveRespond to environment stimuli without memoryAutonomous vacuum cleaners
DeliberativeUse planning to make decisionsSelf-driving cars with route planning
LearningImprove behavior through dataRecommendation systems
HybridCombine various approaches for complex tasksAdvanced virtual assistants

Rule-Based and Reactive Agents

These are the simplest forms of AI agents. They follow predefined rules to respond to inputs. For example, chatbots that answer FAQs or simple automation scripts.

Frequently asked questions about what kind of ai agents can i build

When did AI agents become popular?

AI agents gained popularity in the 2010s with advances in machine learning, especially after the success of virtual assistants like Siri and Alexa.

What are examples of AI agents used today?

Examples include chatbots, autonomous vehicles, virtual assistants, and recommendation engines used by Netflix and Amazon.

Can I build an AI agent without coding experience?

Yes, many platforms offer tools to create AI agents with drag-and-drop interfaces, though complex agents may require programming knowledge.

When will more advanced AI agents come out?

Research continues, and more sophisticated AI agents are expected over the next few years as AI technology advances.
