What Are AI Agents?

AI agents are software entities designed to perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously. They can interpret data, make decisions, and execute actions without human intervention. Examples include chatbots, virtual assistants, and autonomous vehicles.

The core feature of AI agents is their ability to adapt and learn from experience, making them more efficient over time. This adaptability sets them apart from static programs.

  • Autonomous decision-making
  • Learning from data
  • Performing complex tasks
  • Interacting with users or systems

Types of AI Agents

AI agents come in various forms, ranging from simple rule-based systems to complex, learning agents. Reactive agents respond to current inputs, while deliberative agents plan ahead based on stored knowledge.

Some advanced agents incorporate machine learning algorithms to improve their performance continually.
