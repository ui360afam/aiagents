Should I Capitalize Agentic AI? Understanding the Rules

Deciding whether to capitalize 'agentic AI' depends on its usage in your writing. If referring to a specific system or a branded name, capitalization is appropriate. For general references, lowercase is standard. The term 'agentic' describes AI systems capable of autonomous decision-making, akin to human agency.

Style guides like the Associated Press (AP) and Chicago Manual of Style recommend capitalizing proper nouns and titles but advise lowercase for common nouns or adjectives. In technical writing, clarity often guides capitalization decisions.

  • Capitalize when 'Agentic AI' is a proper noun or part of a title.
  • Use lowercase when referring to AI systems in a general sense.
  • Follow your organization's style guide or publication standards.
Use CaseCapitalization
Brand Name or Specific SystemCapitalize (e.g., Agentic AI)
Generic AI systemsLowercase (e.g., agentic AI)
Beginning of sentenceCapitalize (e.g., Agentic AI can enhance automation)

Exceptions and Edge Cases

Some companies or developers might brand their AI systems with capitalized names, such as 'Agentic AI,' to distinguish them. In such cases, use the capitalization consistently. Conversely, 'agentic' as an adjective remains lowercase unless starting a sentence.

When discussing multiple AI systems generically, lowercase is preferred to avoid confusion. For example, 'Many developers are working on agentic AI systems.'

Should I always capitalize agentic AI?

No, only capitalize 'Agentic AI' if it is a proper noun, a brand name, or at the beginning of a sentence. Otherwise, keep it lowercase for general references.

How do I create a free AI agent?

Choose a platform like Rasa or Botpress, follow their tutorials, define your goals, and configure dialogues. Many tools offer free tiers suitable for small projects.

Are there free tools to build AI agents?
