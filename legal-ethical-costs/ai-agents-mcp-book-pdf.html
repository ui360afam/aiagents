Difference Between AI Agents, Bots, and Chatbots

AI agents are general-purpose autonomous programs that can perform a variety of tasks. Bots are a subset of AI agents focused mainly on automating interactions, often in messaging platforms. Chatbots are designed to simulate conversations, but not all chatbots are capable of complex decision-making.

Are AI agents and bots the same? Not exactly. While both can be autonomous, AI agents can handle more complex tasks beyond simple conversation, and not all bots are AI-driven.
