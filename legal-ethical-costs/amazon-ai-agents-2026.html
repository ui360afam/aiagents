How Do Amazon AI Agents Work?

Amazon AI agents use natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to interpret user requests. They analyze inputs, determine intent, and generate relevant responses or actions.

Are AI Agents Free to Use and How Do They Differ from Other AI Tools?

Many AI agents, especially those offered by Amazon, come with free tiers or are entirely free to use for basic features. Advanced functionalities might require subscriptions or pay-per-use models.

Are AI agents and bots the same? Not quite. AI agents are autonomous programs capable of performing tasks, whereas bots are often simple scripts or programs that execute predefined commands.

Are AI agents and chatbots the same? No. Chatbots typically handle conversations based on scripts, while AI agents can perform a broader range of tasks with more autonomy.
