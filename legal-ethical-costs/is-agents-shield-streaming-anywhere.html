Understanding AI Agents and Their Applications

AI agents are autonomous software entities designed to perform specific tasks. They are used in customer service, virtual assistance, and automation across various industries. These differ from simple chatbots, which often rely on predefined scripts.

For example, AI agents can schedule appointments, analyze data, or automate repetitive tasks, often functioning independently once programmed.

  • Used in customer support chatbots
  • Automate repetitive business tasks
  • Assist in data analysis and decision making
  • Can operate on multiple platforms

Are AI Agents Free to Use?

Many AI agents are available for free, especially open-source projects like Rasa or Botpress. Commercial AI agents often require licensing fees, but free versions are sufficient for small projects or testing.

Are AI Agents and Chatbots the Same?

While both are AI-driven systems, chatbots are a subset of AI agents focused on conversational tasks. AI agents may perform a broader range of functions beyond chatting, such as data processing or automation.
