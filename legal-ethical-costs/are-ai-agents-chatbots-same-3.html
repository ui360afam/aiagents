What Are AI Agents and How Do They Differ From Chatbots?

AI agents are software entities that perform tasks, make decisions, and interact with users or systems based on complex algorithms. They can operate autonomously across various environments and often handle multiple functions simultaneously.

Chatbots are a specific type of AI application designed primarily for conversation. They simulate human dialogue to assist users with tasks like customer support, information retrieval, or simple interactions.

  • AI agents can perform actions beyond chatting, such as controlling smart devices or managing workflows.
  • Chatbots focus mainly on textual or voice interactions for communication purposes.
  • AI agents may incorporate multiple AI techniques, including machine learning, planning, and reasoning.
  • Chatbots generally rely on predefined scripts or natural language processing for responses.

Differences in Functionality

AI agents are often integrated into larger systems, handling tasks like data analysis or automation. They can learn from interactions and adapt over time.

Chatbots are optimized for communication, often using rule-based systems or simple NLP models. They may lack the autonomous decision-making capabilities of AI agents.
