What Are AI Agents and Why They Matter

AI agents are software entities that perform tasks autonomously using artificial intelligence. They can interact with users, analyze data, and make decisions, often improving productivity and accuracy.

They matter because they automate complex processes, reduce manual effort, and enable real-time decision making. Businesses increasingly rely on AI agents for customer support, data analysis, and operational automation.

  • Automate repetitive tasks
  • Enhance decision-making
  • Improve customer engagement
  • Support data-driven strategies

Are AI Agents Free to Use?

Many AI agents are available as open source tools, making them free to use and modify. Examples include Rasa, Botpress, and OpenAI's GPT models. However, some advanced or enterprise-level AI agents may require licensing fees.

Are AI Agents and Chatbots the Same?

No, chatbots are a type of AI agent designed mainly for conversational tasks. AI agents can perform broader functions, including data processing, automation, and decision making, beyond just conversation.

How AI Agents Work

AI agents combine natural language processing, machine learning, and rule-based algorithms to perform tasks. They process inputs, analyze data, and decide on actions based on programmed objectives.

Most AI agents operate through APIs or integrations within platforms. They learn from data, adapt over time, and can be trained to improve their performance.

  • Input processing via natural language understanding
  • Decision making using machine learning models
  • Execution of tasks through APIs or automation platforms
  • Continuous learning and improvement

Are AI Agents Open Source?
