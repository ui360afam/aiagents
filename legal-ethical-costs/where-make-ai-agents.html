Popular Platforms to Make AI Agents

Many developers and companies turn to established platforms for creating AI agents. These platforms offer tools, APIs, and frameworks to build, train, and deploy AI agents efficiently. Examples include Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud AI, and Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Each platform provides different features, pricing models, and levels of customization, so choosing the right one depends on your specific needs and technical expertise.

  • Microsoft Azure Bot Service offers integrated tools for building AI agents with natural language understanding.
  • Google Cloud AI provides customizable models and APIs for creating AI agents that can handle complex tasks.
  • AWS offers a broad set of AI and machine learning tools suitable for scaling AI agents.

Open Source Tools for AI Agents

Open source platforms give full control over AI agent development without licensing costs. Popular options include Rasa, Botpress, and OpenAI's GPT models. These tools enable customization and integration into existing systems.

Rasa, for example, is a framework specifically designed for building conversational AI and chatbots, but it can also support more autonomous AI agent functionalities.

Are AI Agents and Bots the Same?

AI agents and chatbots often get confused, but they serve different functions. A chatbot is a conversational interface designed to simulate human dialogue, often limited to predefined scripts.

An AI agent, by contrast, is an autonomous system capable of performing complex tasks beyond conversation, such as data analysis, decision making, or automation, often powered by AI models like GPT.

  • Chatbots typically handle simple interactions and FAQs.
  • AI agents can operate independently, making decisions based on data and AI algorithms.
  • Some AI agents include conversational capabilities, blurring the lines between the two.

Are AI Agents Free to Use?

Many AI agent development tools and platforms offer free tiers or open source licenses. For example, Rasa and Botpress are completely free, while cloud providers like AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure provide free credits to get started.

However, deploying AI agents at scale or using advanced features often incurs costs. It's essential to evaluate your project's scope and budget before choosing a platform.

  • Open source tools are generally free but require technical skills.
  • Cloud platforms may offer free tiers, but high usage can become costly.
  • Always check licensing and pricing details before starting development.

Are AI Agents on Moltbook?

It is advisable to review Moltbook's current offerings, community support, and whether it aligns with your development goals.

Frequently asked questions about where to make ai agents

Can I create an AI agent without coding experience?

Yes, some platforms like Botpress and many cloud services offer visual builders and templates that require minimal coding. However, customizing complex AI agents often requires programming skills.

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot is mainly designed for conversation, while an AI agent can perform a broader range of tasks autonomously, such as data processing and decision making, often using AI models.

Are open source AI agent tools secure?

Open source tools are as secure as their implementation. Proper security practices and regular updates are essential. Open source platforms also benefit from community reviews that help identify vulnerabilities.

Is it possible to make AI agents for free?

Yes, many open source tools and free tiers from cloud providers enable developing AI agents without upfront costs. Advanced features and large-scale deployment may require payment.

Can I deploy AI agents on existing websites?

Yes, most platforms provide APIs and integration options to embed AI agents into websites, apps, or messaging platforms, enabling seamless user interactions.

The bottom line

Knowing where to make AI agents involves evaluating available platforms, their features, costs, and your own technical capabilities. Start with open source tools if customization is essential, or leverage cloud providers for ease of deployment. The next step is to select a platform aligned with your goals and begin building your AI agent today.
