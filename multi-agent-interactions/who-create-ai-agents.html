Who Creates AI Agents? A Clear Explanation

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 3 min read

These creators develop AI agents for applications like customer support, automation, and data analysis. Understanding who creates AI agents helps clarify their capabilities and limitations.

Who Creates AI Agents?

AI agents are primarily created by software engineers, data scientists, and artificial intelligence researchers. These professionals develop code, design algorithms, and train models to perform specific tasks. Major technology companies such as Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI invest heavily in creating AI systems. Startups and academic institutions also contribute to AI agent development, often focusing on specialized applications.

In addition to large corporations, open-source communities play a role by releasing frameworks and tools that enable others to create AI agents. These tools include TensorFlow, PyTorch, and OpenAI's Gym. Developers use these platforms to build, test, and deploy AI agents tailored to various industries.

How Are AI Agents Created?

Creating an AI agent involves several steps. First, developers define the task or problem the agent will address. Then, they gather and prepare training data or design rules for the system. Machine learning models are trained using this data to recognize patterns and make decisions. The process continues with testing and refinement to improve accuracy and efficiency.

Developers also integrate APIs, databases, and other tools to enhance the AI agent’s capabilities. The final product is deployed in a real-world environment or embedded within applications. This process can take from weeks to months, depending on complexity.

Can AI Agents Interact With Each Other?

Yes, AI agents can interact with each other, especially in multi-agent systems. These systems are designed where multiple AI agents communicate to achieve complex goals, such as coordinating in robotics or managing network traffic. Communication protocols, such as message passing or shared environments, facilitate these interactions.

For example, in a customer service chatbot system, different AI agents might handle inquiries, escalate issues, or provide recommendations by exchanging information. Developers can program AI agents to 'talk to each other' through specific commands or APIs.

How To Make Two AI Agents Talk To Each Other

To make two AI agents converse, developers set up communication channels, often through APIs or message queues. They encode messages in a structured format, like JSON, and define protocols so each agent understands the other's language. The agents then send and receive messages, adjusting their behavior based on the exchanged information.

Exceptions and Edge Cases in AI Agent Creation

While many AI agents are created by developers, some are the result of automated systems or evolutionary algorithms that generate code or behaviors independently. In research settings, AI agents may evolve in simulated environments without direct human intervention. These cases are edge cases and less common in commercial applications.

Another exception involves open-source projects where communities contribute to creating AI agents collectively. Here, the 'creators' are many, often volunteers, and the process is decentralized. This broad participation can lead to diverse, sometimes unpredictable, AI agent behaviors.

Frequently asked questions about who create ai agents

Can AI agents create other AI agents?

Yes, some advanced AI systems can generate designs or code for new AI agents, often using meta-learning or automated machine learning techniques. However, human oversight remains essential for ensuring safety and effectiveness.

How do I make AI agents talk to each other?

You set up communication protocols using APIs, message queues, or shared data environments. Define message formats, establish channels, and program each agent to send and process messages accordingly.

Are there limits to AI agent interaction?

Yes, interaction depends on the design and protocols. Some AI agents are programmed only to perform specific tasks independently and do not communicate with others. Complex multi-agent systems require careful planning of interaction rules.

Who funds the development of AI agents?

Funding comes from tech giants, government research grants, startups, and academic institutions. Large corporations invest billions annually in AI research and development.

Can AI agents learn from each other?

Yes, in multi-agent learning models, agents can adapt based on interactions with others, leading to improved performance over time. This is common in reinforcement learning setups.

The bottom line

Understanding who creates AI agents clarifies their capabilities and limitations. As AI systems evolve, the focus shifts toward enhancing collaboration and ensuring ethical development.
