Is Agentic AI and AI Agents the Same Thing?

  • Agentic AI exhibits decision-making similar to human agency.
  • Communication between AI agents requires standardized protocols.

Understanding AI Agents and Agentic AI

AI agents are software programs designed to perform specific tasks without human intervention. Examples include virtual assistants like Siri or chatbots. Agentic AI, on the other hand, refers to AI systems that demonstrate a degree of human-like agency, making independent decisions based on complex data.

While all agentic AI can be considered AI agents, not all AI agents are agentic. Many AI systems operate within tightly controlled parameters and lack the autonomy or decision-making capabilities associated with agentic AI.

  • AI agents follow programmed instructions or learned behaviors.
  • Agentic AI can set goals and adapt strategies independently.
  • The distinction lies in decision-making autonomy and goal-setting.

Can AI Agents Interact With Each Other?

Yes, AI agents can interact with each other. Multi-agent systems are designed for such communication, enabling collaborative problem-solving, resource sharing, and coordinated actions.

For example, autonomous vehicles communicate to optimize traffic flow, and chatbots can exchange information to better assist users. However, the complexity of interaction depends on the system's design and purpose.

How to Make Two AI Agents Talk to Each Other

Implement communication protocols such as REST APIs, WebSockets, or custom message passing systems. Define clear data formats and message structures for effective communication.

Ensure each agent can interpret messages and respond appropriately. Use middleware or frameworks like OpenAI's GPT API or multi-agent platforms to facilitate interaction.

Edge Cases and Exceptions in AI Agent Communication

While many systems support AI interaction, some scenarios pose challenges. For instance, incompatible message formats or differing system architectures can hinder communication.

In multi-agent systems designed for negotiation or competition, agents may withhold information or act adversarially, complicating cooperation. Ensuring standardized protocols and robust security measures is vital.

When Do AI Agents Fail to Communicate?

Failures occur if there is a mismatch in communication protocols, or if agents are designed with incompatible architectures. Network issues or security restrictions can also prevent interaction.

Designers must establish common standards and test communication channels thoroughly to prevent these issues.

Practical Examples of AI Agents and Agentic AI

In practice, AI agents are used in logistics, finance, and customer service. For example, multi-agent systems in supply chain management coordinate inventory and shipping.

Agentic AI is used in autonomous robots and decision-making systems that require independent action, such as Mars rovers or financial trading algorithms.

  • Supply chain multi-agent systems optimize inventory levels.
  • Autonomous vehicles exhibit agentic AI behaviors.
  • Financial algorithms make independent trading decisions.

Frequently asked questions about is agentic ai and ai agents the same thing

Is agentic AI the same as AI agents?

No, they are related but different concepts. AI agents are autonomous programs performing tasks, while agentic AI specifically refers to AI systems that demonstrate human-like decision-making and agency.

Can AI agents communicate with each other?

Yes, AI agents can be programmed to interact using communication protocols like APIs or message passing, enabling collaboration and complex problem-solving.

How do I make two AI agents talk to each other?

Implement communication protocols such as WebSockets or APIs, define message formats, and ensure each agent can interpret and respond to messages for effective interaction.

What are the challenges in AI agent communication?

Challenges include incompatible protocols, security restrictions, network issues, and designing agents to understand and interpret messages properly.

Are agentic AI systems capable of independent decision-making?

Yes, agentic AI systems are designed to make decisions and set goals independently, often based on complex data and learning algorithms.

The bottom line

Understanding the differences between agentic AI and AI agents clarifies their capabilities and limitations. To leverage these systems effectively, focus on designing robust communication protocols and clear goal-setting mechanisms.
