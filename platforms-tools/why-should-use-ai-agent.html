Why Should I Use an AI Agent? Benefits and Insights

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 3 min read

Using an AI agent can significantly improve your productivity by automating routine tasks and providing intelligent assistance. Many users wonder why they should incorporate AI agents into their workflows. This article explains the benefits, how to implement them, and what to consider.

Key takeaways

  • AI agents automate repetitive tasks, saving time and effort.
  • Implementing AI requires understanding platform capabilities and limitations.
  • Use AI as a tool to augment human decision-making, not replace it.
  • Proper configuration and oversight are essential for effective AI use.
  • Many platforms support easy creation of custom AI agents.

Benefits of Using an AI Agent

AI agents automate repetitive and time-consuming tasks, allowing users to focus on strategic activities. For example, automating email responses or data entry can save up to 30 minutes per task.

They enhance decision-making by providing data-driven insights and can operate 24/7 without fatigue, unlike human workers. This leads to increased productivity and faster project completion.

  • Automate routine tasks such as scheduling, data entry, and email responses
  • Provide real-time insights for better decision-making
  • Work continuously without breaks or fatigue
  • Reduce operational costs by minimizing manual labor

Real-World Examples

Many companies use AI agents for customer support, with chatbots handling over 70% of inquiries without human intervention.

Content creators automate social media posts using AI agents integrated with platforms like Obsidian or ChatGPT.

How to Use AI Agents in ChatGPT and Copilot

In ChatGPT, users can create custom AI agents by setting specific instructions or integrating plugins that automate tasks such as summarization or content creation.

Microsoft Copilot allows users to develop AI agents that assist with coding, document editing, and data analysis directly within Office applications.

  • Create AI agents in ChatGPT by defining prompts and workflows
  • Use Copilot to automate coding and document tasks
  • Integrate AI agents with third-party tools for customized workflows
  • Leverage plugins and extensions to expand capabilities

Creating an AI Agent in ChatGPT

Start by defining a clear goal for the AI agent, such as summarizing articles or drafting emails. Use prompts that guide the AI to perform specific tasks.

Incorporate tools like Zapier or custom scripts to automate multi-step workflows.

Using AI Agents in Copilot

Enable Copilot within your Office tools, then customize its functions by setting parameters for coding suggestions or document formatting.

Combine Copilot with data sources like Excel or Power BI for enhanced automation.

Using AI Agents with Obsidian and Other Platforms

Obsidian users can integrate AI agents to enhance knowledge management, automate note summarization, or generate ideas based on existing data.

Other platforms like Notion or Zapier also support AI integrations, broadening automation possibilities.

  • Automate note organization and summarization in Obsidian
  • Create custom workflows with Zapier and AI tools
  • Enhance productivity with AI-powered templates
  • Use AI for research and content generation

Creating AI Automation in Obsidian

Utilize plugins such as Obsidian AI to generate summaries, brainstorm ideas, or perform text analysis within your notes.

Link Obsidian with other automation tools for seamless workflows.

Edge Cases and Exceptions

AI agents are less effective in highly specialized or creative tasks that require human judgment, such as legal analysis or art design.

They also may produce errors if not properly configured, so human oversight remains essential.

Data privacy and security concerns can limit AI deployment in sensitive environments.

  • Avoid relying solely on AI for critical decision-making
  • Monitor AI outputs regularly for accuracy
  • Ensure data privacy when integrating AI agents
  • Use AI as an assistant, not a replacement

When Not to Use AI Agents

In situations requiring nuanced understanding or ethical judgments, human expertise remains irreplaceable. Examples include legal disputes or sensitive negotiations.

If data security is a concern, carefully evaluate AI platform compliance before use.

Frequently asked questions about why should i use an ai agent

Can I create my own AI agent in ChatGPT?

Yes, you can create a custom AI agent in ChatGPT by defining specific prompts, workflows, and integrating third-party tools to automate tasks aligned with your needs.

How do I use AI agents in Copilot?

In Copilot, activate the feature within your Office applications and customize its functions for coding, drafting, or data analysis, often through setting preferences or workflows.

Are AI agents suitable for small businesses?

Absolutely. AI agents can automate customer support, scheduling, and data handling, saving time and reducing costs for small businesses.

What are the risks of using AI agents?

Risks include data security issues, potential errors in outputs, and over-reliance on automation, emphasizing the importance of human oversight and careful configuration.

How can I improve AI agent performance?

Provide clear, specific prompts, regularly review outputs, and update workflows to adapt to changing needs. Integrate feedback for continuous improvement.

The bottom line

Integrating AI agents into your workflows can deliver substantial productivity gains and operational efficiencies. Start by identifying tasks you want to automate, then choose the right platform to build your AI agent. Testing and regular oversight ensure optimal performance. Begin exploring AI agent creation today to enhance your work processes.
