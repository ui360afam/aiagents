What is Generative AI and Agentic AI Explained

Understanding Generative AI and Agentic AI

Generative AI systems, such as GPT models, generate human-like text, images, or sounds by analyzing large datasets. They are designed to produce novel outputs based on learned patterns.

Agentic AI refers to autonomous systems that can perform tasks, make decisions, and adapt without human intervention. These agents often operate within frameworks that allow them to pursue specific goals.

While generative AI focuses on content creation, agentic AI emphasizes autonomous decision-making. The two can overlap when generative models are embedded within autonomous agents.

  • Generative AI creates new data outputs from existing data.
  • Agentic AI can operate independently in complex environments.
  • Combining both leads to smarter, more autonomous systems.

Differences Between Generative and Agentic AI

Generative AI strictly produces content, such as writing articles or designing images, based on input prompts.

Agentic AI has decision-making capabilities, allowing it to plan, execute, and adapt to new information without constant human input.

For example, a generative AI might write a story, while an agentic AI could manage a customer support chatbot that learns from interactions.

How to Use AI Agents in ChatGPT and Copilot

In ChatGPT, AI agents can be created by designing prompts that guide the model’s behavior or by integrating custom plugins that extend its capabilities.

Microsoft's Copilot uses AI agents embedded within productivity tools like Word or Excel, automating tasks such as data analysis or content summarization.

To create an AI agent in ChatGPT, developers can use OpenAI's API to build applications that perform specific tasks automatically, such as customer support or data entry.

In Copilot, AI agents are pre-built into the platform but can be customized using scripts or integrations to suit particular workflows.

  • Use API calls to develop custom AI agents in ChatGPT.
  • Integrate AI agents into productivity tools like Copilot.
  • Leverage plugins and extensions for more advanced capabilities.

Creating and Training AI Agents

Creating an AI agent involves selecting the right model, defining its tasks, and training or configuring it with appropriate data.

In ChatGPT or Copilot, you can start by customizing prompts, integrating APIs, or fine-tuning models with specific datasets.

Edge cases include handling ambiguous inputs, ensuring data privacy, and managing unexpected AI behaviors.

  • Choose the right AI model for your needs.
  • Define clear goals and input parameters.
  • Test extensively to handle edge cases like ambiguous instructions.

Edge Cases and Limitations of AI Agents

AI agents may produce unpredictable outputs when encountering ambiguous or out-of-distribution inputs.

Handling privacy-sensitive data requires careful safeguards, especially when deploying autonomous agents.

Limitations include biases present in training data, inability to understand context fully, and potential security vulnerabilities.

  • Edge cases include confusing prompts or adversarial inputs.
  • Always implement safety checks and validation.
  • Regularly audit AI outputs for biases and inaccuracies.

Examples of Edge Cases

An AI agent misinterpreting a sarcastic remark as a serious instruction.

Unintended data leaks when APIs are improperly secured.

Biases in generated content reflecting training data biases.

Frequently asked questions about what is generative ai and agentic ai

What is the main difference between generative AI and agentic AI?

Generative AI creates content based on input data, while agentic AI performs tasks autonomously, making decisions without human input. Both can be combined for advanced automation.

How can I create an AI agent in ChatGPT?

Start by obtaining API access, design prompts or scripts that specify tasks, and integrate these into your application. Fine-tune prompts based on performance to improve results.

Can I use AI agents with Obsidian?

Yes, through plugins and custom scripts, you can integrate AI agents into Obsidian for tasks like summarization, content generation, or knowledge retrieval.

What are common limitations of AI agents?

Limitations include handling ambiguous inputs, biases in generated content, privacy concerns, and security vulnerabilities. Proper testing and safeguards are essential.

How do I train or fine-tune an AI agent?

Use specific datasets to fine-tune models, adjust parameters, and continuously monitor outputs. For custom tasks, training with relevant data improves accuracy.

The bottom line

Understanding what is generative AI and agentic AI enables effective deployment in various applications. Start experimenting with creating custom AI agents in platforms like ChatGPT, Copilot, or Obsidian, and monitor their performance to optimize results.
