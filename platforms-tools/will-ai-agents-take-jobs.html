Will AI Agents Take Jobs? Understanding the Impact

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 3 min read

The question of whether AI agents will take jobs is common among workers and employers alike. While AI can automate certain tasks, it also creates new opportunities for collaboration and innovation. Understanding how AI agents work helps clarify their impact on employment.

  • Most jobs will evolve rather than disappear due to AI.
  • Using AI in ChatGPT, Copilot, and Obsidian enhances productivity.

Will AI Agents Take Jobs? The Reality

AI agents are designed to automate repetitive and routine tasks, which means some jobs may change or become obsolete. According to recent studies, between 15% and 30% of roles could see significant automation within the next decade.

However, AI also introduces new roles. Jobs involving complex decision-making, emotional intelligence, and creative problem-solving are less likely to be fully replaced. Instead, many workers will see AI as a tool that enhances productivity.

  • Automation affects tasks, not entire jobs
  • AI enhances worker productivity and decision-making
  • Certain sectors face higher automation risk

Jobs Most at Risk

Roles involving data entry, basic customer interactions, and routine manufacturing are most vulnerable. For example, customer service bots have replaced many entry-level support jobs.

In manufacturing, repetitive assembly line tasks are increasingly handled by robots and AI systems.

Jobs Less Likely to Be Replaced

Jobs requiring complex judgment, emotional intelligence, and strategic thinking remain resilient. Healthcare providers, educators, and creative professionals are less impacted.

AI can assist these roles but cannot fully replace the human element.

How to Use AI Agents in ChatGPT and Copilot

Using AI agents effectively involves integrating them into workflows. In ChatGPT, users can create custom AI agents by adding plugins or training specific prompts. For example, an AI agent can handle customer inquiries automatically.

In GitHub Copilot, users configure workflows that generate code snippets, automate routine coding tasks, and improve software development speed. Both platforms enable users to create AI agents that fit their needs.

  • Integrate plugins in ChatGPT for specialized tasks
  • Create custom prompts for tailored AI behavior
  • Configure workflows in Copilot for automation
  • Use Obsidian with AI agents for knowledge management

Using AI Agents in ChatGPT

To create an AI agent in ChatGPT, start by defining its purpose. Use prompts, plugins, or APIs to customize responses. For example, a travel planning AI can gather information and book arrangements.

You can also connect ChatGPT to external services, making the AI more versatile.

Creating AI Agents in Copilot

In Copilot, setting up an AI agent involves writing specific code workflows. For instance, automating repetitive coding tasks or generating boilerplate code saves time.

Adjust settings to refine AI suggestions based on project requirements.

Using Obsidian with AI Agents

Obsidian users can integrate AI agents to enhance note-taking, automate tagging, or generate summaries. Connecting AI to Obsidian involves plugins that interface with AI APIs.

This setup improves knowledge management efficiency.

Edge Cases and Exceptions

Some jobs may see only partial automation, where AI handles specific tasks but requires human oversight. For example, AI might draft legal documents but needs a lawyer to review and finalize.

Emerging AI capabilities may also create entirely new roles, such as AI trainers or ethical AI auditors. These positions require specialized skills and knowledge.

  • Human oversight remains essential in high-stakes roles
  • AI can assist but not fully replace strategic decision-making
  • New roles emerge as AI technology develops

Partial Automation Examples

In finance, AI can analyze data and generate reports, but analysts interpret the findings. Similarly, in healthcare, AI suggests diagnoses but doctors make final decisions.

These hybrid roles combine AI efficiency with human expertise.

Emerging Job Roles

As AI becomes more sophisticated, jobs like AI ethics consultants, AI trainers, and data annotators will grow. These roles ensure AI systems operate fairly and accurately.

Training AI models often requires domain-specific knowledge and oversight.

Frequently asked questions about will ai agents take jobs

Will AI agents completely replace human workers?

While AI agents can automate many routine tasks, complete replacement of human workers is unlikely in the near future. Many roles still require human judgment, emotional intelligence, and creativity.

How can I start using AI agents in my work?

Begin by identifying repetitive tasks and explore AI tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, or Obsidian plugins. Set up workflows, create custom prompts, or integrate APIs to automate and enhance your tasks.

Which jobs are most at risk from AI automation?

Jobs involving repetitive tasks such as data entry, basic customer support, and simple manufacturing are most at risk. Roles requiring complex judgment or emotional skills are less vulnerable.

Can AI agents improve productivity without replacing jobs?

Yes, AI agents often serve as tools that augment human abilities, allowing workers to focus on higher-value tasks and reduce manual effort.

What are the best practices for creating AI agents in ChatGPT or Copilot?

Define clear objectives, customize prompts or workflows, test extensively, and monitor performance regularly. Use available plugins, APIs, and tutorials to optimize your AI agents.

The bottom line

AI agents are transforming workplaces by automating tasks and enhancing productivity. Instead of fearing job loss, focus on learning how to create and use AI tools effectively. Start exploring AI integrations today to stay competitive.
