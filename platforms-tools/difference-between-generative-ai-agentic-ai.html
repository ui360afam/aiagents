Difference Between Generative AI & Agentic AI: Complete Guide

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 4 min read

Understanding the difference between generative AI and agentic AI is essential for anyone working with artificial intelligence today. These two types of AI serve different purposes and have distinct capabilities. Generative AI creates content based on input, such as text or images, while agentic AI acts autonomously to perform tasks or make decisions. Knowing how each works helps you select the right tools for your projects.

Key takeaways

  • Generative AI focuses on content creation, while agentic AI handles autonomous decision-making.
  • Knowing the difference helps you choose the right AI tools for your project.
  • You can create AI agents in platforms like ChatGPT, Copilot, and Obsidian to automate tasks.
  • Regularly monitor AI outputs to ensure quality and relevance.

What Is Generative AI and How Does It Work?

Generative AI refers to models that produce new data similar to their training data. These models, including GPT-4 and DALL·E, analyze large datasets to learn patterns and generate content such as text, images, or audio. They respond to prompts by creating original outputs based on learned structures.

For example, using ChatGPT to generate a blog post or using DALL·E to create visual art involves generative AI. It is primarily focused on content creation without autonomous decision-making.

  • Uses deep learning models trained on vast datasets
  • Generates text, images, or audio based on prompts
  • Examples include ChatGPT, Midjourney, and GPT-4

Main purposeCreate new content based on input
Decision-makingLimited; generates outputs based on prompts
AutonomyDepends on user input; not fully autonomous

How Generative AI Is Used Today

Generative AI is widely used in content creation, marketing, and design. Content creators use ChatGPT for writing assistance, while artists use AI tools like Midjourney to generate visuals. ChatGPT in customer support automates responses, saving time and effort.

What Is Agentic AI and How Does It Work?

Agentic AI refers to systems that operate with a level of autonomy, making decisions and completing tasks without constant human input. These AI agents are programmed with goals and rules that guide their actions. They can adapt to changing environments and perform complex workflows.

For example, an AI agent integrated into a customer support system can automatically handle inquiries, escalate issues, or even initiate follow-up actions based on the context. These agents are often used in automation platforms like Copilot or Obsidian with AI plugins.

  • Autonomous decision-making based on goals
  • Can execute multi-step tasks without direct input
  • Examples include AI assistants in Copilot and Obsidian

How Agentic AI Is Applied

Agentic AI is used in automation, robotics, and complex decision systems. In programming, AI agents in GitHub Copilot can suggest code snippets and complete functions. In knowledge management, AI agents in Obsidian automate note organization and retrieval.

Why The Difference Matters

Distinguishing between generative and agentic AI helps you select the right tools for your needs. For content creation, generative AI provides rapid, high-quality outputs. For automation and decision-making, agentic AI reduces manual effort and improves efficiency.

Understanding these differences prevents misuse and helps you implement AI systems effectively, whether you are building chatbots, automating workflows, or creating new content.

  • Choosing the correct AI type saves time and resources
  • Improves system performance and reliability
  • Aligns AI capabilities with project goals

How To Use AI Agents in ChatGPT, Copilot, and Obsidian

Integrating AI agents into your workflows can significantly enhance productivity. For example, in ChatGPT, you can create custom AI agents by designing prompts and behavior scripts. In Copilot, AI assists with code suggestions and automation tasks. Obsidian users can incorporate AI plugins to automate note management.

  • Create AI agents in ChatGPT by defining prompts and rules
  • Use Copilot to automate coding tasks with AI suggestions
  • Implement AI plugins in Obsidian for note automation

Creating an AI Agent in ChatGPT

To create an AI agent in ChatGPT, define clear prompts and behaviors. Use system instructions to set the tone and task scope. For more advanced setups, use API integrations to automate interactions and develop multi-step workflows.

Using AI Agents in Copilot

In GitHub Copilot, AI acts as an assistant by suggesting code snippets as you type. You can configure settings for specific languages or project types to improve suggestions and automate repetitive coding tasks.

Using Obsidian with AI Agents

Obsidian offers plugins like AI Assistant that automate note organization, tagging, and retrieval. Setting up involves installing plugins, connecting to AI services, and customizing workflows to match your note-taking style.

Common Mistakes and How To Avoid Them

Misunderstanding the scope of AI capabilities can lead to ineffective implementations. Relying solely on generative AI for decision-making or assuming AI agents will fully replace human judgment can cause issues.

To avoid these mistakes, clearly define the purpose of your AI system, set realistic expectations, and continuously monitor performance. Test AI outputs regularly to ensure accuracy and relevance.

  • Avoid overestimating AI autonomy
  • Set clear boundaries and goals
  • Regularly review AI outputs for quality
  • Train and update models with relevant data

What is the main difference between generative AI and agentic AI?

Generative AI creates new content based on input data, while agentic AI performs autonomous tasks and decisions guided by goals and rules.

Yes, you can create custom AI agents in ChatGPT by designing prompts and workflows, allowing it to perform specific tasks autonomously.

Understanding the difference between generative AI and agentic AI empowers you to leverage these technologies effectively. Start experimenting with creating AI agents in platforms like ChatGPT or Copilot to automate workflows and produce content efficiently. Next, explore integrating AI into your existing systems to maximize productivity.
