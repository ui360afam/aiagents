Frequently asked questions about best way to make money with ai agents

How can I learn to create an AI agent in ChatGPT?

Start by exploring the ChatGPT API documentation, learn prompt engineering, and experiment with scripting to develop tailored AI agents for various tasks.

What skills do I need to monetize AI agents?

You should understand AI platform APIs, scripting, basic programming, and have knowledge of the target industry where you plan to apply or sell AI solutions.

Can I use AI agents in Obsidian to make money?

Yes, by automating note-taking, summarization, and knowledge management, you can create valuable tools for researchers or content creators and monetize these services.

What are the main drawbacks of using AI agents for business?

AI agents can produce errors or misunderstandings, require ongoing training, and may lack the emotional intelligence needed for certain customer interactions.

How much money can I make with AI agents?

Earnings vary widely; simple projects might earn a few hundred dollars, while custom AI solutions for clients can generate thousands per project depending on scope and complexity.
