Should Agentic AI Be Capitalized in Writing

By Editorial Team · Updated 2026-09-16 · 3 min read

When referring to AI agents, many writers wonder if the phrase 'agentic AI' should be capitalized. This guide clarifies the capitalization rules, explains when to capitalize, and provides practical examples for using AI agents in various platforms like ChatGPT, Copilot, and Obsidian. Proper capitalization ensures clarity and professionalism in technical writing.

Key takeaways

  • Use lowercase 'agentic AI' in most writing unless it is a proper noun.

Should Agentic AI Be Capitalized in General Usage

In standard writing, the phrase 'agentic AI' is typically not capitalized. It functions as a common noun phrase describing a type of artificial intelligence with agency. For example, 'This system employs agentic AI to automate tasks.'

However, when referring to a specific product, brand, or a proprietary name, capitalization is appropriate. For example, 'We integrated Agentic AI in our workflow.'

  • Use lowercase in general descriptions.
  • Capitalize when referring to specific named systems.
  • Maintain consistency throughout your document.
SituationExample
General descriptionThis system uses agentic AI to improve automation.
Specific product nameWe are testing Agentic AI in Copilot.
Branding or proprietary termAgentic AI Suite is available now.

When to Capitalize 'Agentic AI'

Capitalize 'Agentic AI' if it is part of a proper noun or product name, such as 'Agentic AI Platform' or 'Agentic AI in Copilot.'

If you create a branded or proprietary term that includes 'Agentic AI,' capitalize accordingly.

How to Use AI Agents Correctly in Writing

Proper use of 'agentic AI' involves understanding the context. When discussing AI agents in platforms like ChatGPT or Copilot, it's essential to follow consistent capitalization rules.

For instance, in technical documentation, referring to 'using AI agents in ChatGPT' keeps the phrase lowercase unless it is a formal name.

  • Use lowercase for generic mentions of AI agents.
  • Capitalize when referring to specific systems or branded tools.
  • Always check the style guide or brand guidelines for consistency.

Using AI Agents in ChatGPT

When describing how to use AI agents in ChatGPT, write 'using AI agents in ChatGPT' with lowercase, unless you have a specific branded version.

Example: 'Learn how to create an AI agent in ChatGPT for automating responses.'

Using AI Agents in Copilot

In documentation about GitHub Copilot, you might write 'how to create an AI agent in Copilot' or 'integrate AI agents in Copilot.'

Capitalize 'Copilot' as it is a proper noun, but keep 'AI agent' lowercase unless referring to a branded system.

Creating and Using AI Agents with Obsidian

Obsidian users can integrate AI agents through plugins and scripts. When documenting this process, maintain consistent terminology and capitalization.

For example, 'How to use Obsidian with AI agents' is correct, with lowercase unless referring to a specific branded AI agent.

  • Describe steps clearly: 'Install plugin,' 'Configure AI agent,' 'Use in Obsidian.'
  • Capitalize 'Obsidian' as it is a product name, lowercase 'AI agents' in general context.

Examples of AI agent integration in Obsidian

You might write: 'Integrate AI agents in Obsidian to enhance note management,' or 'Create an AI agent in Obsidian to automate tagging.'

Edge Cases and Exceptions in Capitalization

Some edge cases involve branding, trademarks, or proprietary names. If a company names its AI system 'Agentic AI' as a product, capitalize the phrase consistently.

In academic or technical writing, follow style guides like APA or Chicago Manual of Style, which typically recommend lowercase unless the term is a proper noun.

  • Brand names: 'Agentic AI' or 'Agentic AI Platform.'
  • Common nouns: 'agentic AI' when generic.
  • Consistency is key in all cases.

Capitalization in branding

When a company brands its AI system as 'Agentic AI,' always capitalize it in all instances to maintain brand consistency.

Frequently asked questions about should agentic ai be capitalized

Should I capitalize 'agentic AI' in a research paper?

In most cases, 'agentic AI' should be lowercase unless it is part of a specific product name or brand. Follow your style guide for consistency.

Is 'Agentic AI' a proper noun?

It is not a proper noun unless used as a branded or proprietary term. Use lowercase in general references.

How do I refer to AI agents in technical documentation?

Use lowercase 'AI agents' unless explicitly naming a product or system that requires capitalization.

Can I capitalize 'agentic AI' when talking about its capabilities?

Only capitalize if it is part of a formal name. Otherwise, keep it lowercase for clarity.

Are there any exceptions to lowercase 'agentic AI'?

Yes, when referring to branded products, proprietary systems, or specific titles, capitalize accordingly.

The bottom line

Understanding when to capitalize 'agentic AI' helps ensure clarity and professionalism in your writing. Always check whether the term refers to a specific product or a general concept. For next steps, review your organization's style guide or consult branding guidelines for proprietary names.
