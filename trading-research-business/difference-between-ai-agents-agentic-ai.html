Understanding AI Agents and Agentic AI

AI agents are programs created to perform specific tasks, such as data analysis, customer service, or trading. They operate based on predefined rules or learning algorithms. In contrast, agentic AI possesses a higher level of autonomy and can set its own goals, adapt strategies, and make complex decisions.

  • AI agents follow programmed instructions or learned patterns.
  • Agentic AI demonstrates autonomous goal setting.
  • Examples include chatbots versus advanced decision-making systems.
FeatureAI AgentsAgentic AI
AutonomyLimited, follows scripts or learned patternsHigh, sets own goals and strategies
Decision MakingPredefined rules or trainingAdaptive, can evaluate and choose actions

Examples of AI Agents

A chatbot on a website that answers customer questions is an AI agent. An AI-powered stock trading platform that automatically buys or sells stocks based on market data is another example.

What Makes Agentic AI Different

Agentic AI can evaluate situations, consider multiple options, and choose the best course of action without human intervention. It uses advanced algorithms, sometimes incorporating elements of artificial general intelligence, to mimic human decision-making.

How to Create an AI Agent for Trading Stocks

Creating an AI agent to trade stocks involves several steps. First, choose a platform or programming language such as Python. Next, gather historical market data. Then, develop algorithms that analyze this data for patterns and signals.

Test your AI trading bot extensively using historical data before deploying it in live markets. Consider risk management techniques such as stop-loss orders and diversify your strategies to improve profitability.

  • Select a programming language like Python or use existing platforms.
  • Collect and preprocess historical stock data.
  • Develop and backtest your trading algorithm.
  • Implement risk controls before live trading.

Edge Cases and Exceptions in AI Agent Behavior

While AI agents and agentic AI can perform complex tasks, they are not infallible. Market crashes, unexpected news, or data errors can cause AI trading bots to malfunction or incur losses.

Some AI systems may behave unpredictably if they encounter scenarios outside their training data. Proper safeguards, continuous monitoring, and updates are essential to prevent significant losses.

  • Market volatility can cause AI trading bots to lose money.
  • Poor data quality affects AI decision accuracy.
  • Unanticipated events require human oversight.

Frequently asked questions about what is difference between ai agents and agentic ai

Can AI agents make money trading stocks?

Yes, AI trading agents can potentially generate profits by analyzing market data and executing trades automatically. However, success depends on strategy quality, market conditions, and risk management.

How do I create an AI agent to trade stocks?

To create a trading AI, select a programming language like Python, gather historical data, develop algorithms based on technical analysis, and backtest extensively before live deployment.

Are AI agents better than humans at trading?

AI agents can process large data sets faster and execute trades more quickly. However, they lack human intuition and can be affected by market anomalies, so combining human oversight is often best.

Can you make money with AI agents on Reddit?

Some Reddit communities discuss AI trading bots and monetization strategies. While there is potential, success depends on strategy, market conditions, and how well the AI is programmed.

What are the risks of using AI agents for trading?

Risks include market volatility, data errors, and unforeseen events. Poorly designed AI can lead to significant losses, so proper safeguards and ongoing monitoring are essential.
