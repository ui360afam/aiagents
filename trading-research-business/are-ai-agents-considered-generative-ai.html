What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that produce new data or content. They learn from large datasets to generate text, images, music, or videos. Examples include GPT-4 for text and DALL-E for images.

These models are trained to understand patterns in data and then create original outputs based on that understanding. Their primary characteristic is content creation, which is different from task automation or decision-making.

  • Creates new content based on learned patterns.
  • Uses models like neural networks trained on large datasets.
  • Examples include text generators and image synthesis tools.

Examples of Generative AI

Popular examples include ChatGPT, which generates human-like text, and Midjourney, which produces artistic images from prompts. These systems can be used to generate articles, artwork, and even code.

Are AI Agents Considered Generative AI?

AI agents are typically not considered generative AI unless their primary function involves content creation. Most AI agents are designed to perform specific tasks such as trading stocks or answering customer queries.

For example, an AI agent that automates stock trading analyzes market data to make buy or sell decisions. This task involves decision-making and pattern recognition, but not content generation, so it is not categorized as generative AI.

  • AI agents can perform automation, decision-making, and problem-solving.
  • They are considered generative AI only if they produce new content.
  • Most AI agents focus on executing tasks based on inputs and learned rules.

Edge Cases and Exceptions

Some AI systems blur the lines between generative and non-generative functions. For instance, AI agents that generate reports or summaries from data may involve content creation, making them partially generative.

In trading, some AI agents generate trading signals or strategies based on historical data, but they do not produce new content like text or images. These are considered decision-making tools rather than generative AI.

  • Content generation vs. pattern recognition
  • Hybrid systems that combine automation with content creation
  • AI agents that create code or reports can be seen as partially generative
