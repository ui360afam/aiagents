Common Mistakes When Using AI Agents

Many beginners make errors that reduce their chances of success with AI agents. Common mistakes include overfitting models, neglecting proper testing, or misunderstanding the scope of AI capabilities.

For instance, relying solely on backtested data without real-time testing can lead to losses. Also, using poorly designed AI agents for trading may result in significant financial risks.

  • Overfitting training data
  • Ignoring market volatility
  • Not updating AI models regularly
  • Relying solely on automated decisions without oversight

Tips to Avoid Common Mistakes

Always perform out-of-sample testing and paper trading before deploying AI agents in real scenarios. Keep your models updated with recent data and monitor their performance continuously.
